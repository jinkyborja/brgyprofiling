Status definitions

  • Existing: a related screen/file is in the legacy app; this does not imply a role-specific portal or complete workflow.
  • Partial / Incomplete: a related screen exists, but requested behavior, data workflow, or account policy is incomplete.
  • Missing: the requested page or workflow is not implemented in the legacy PHP app. A separate mock design is provided when marked “Designed”.
  • Designed: a sample-data UI screen can be opened locally. It is not connected to PHP authentication or MySQL.
  • Responsive smoke checked: the page was opened at the five listed viewport widths and checked for page-level horizontal overflow. It does not certify every visual or accessibility detail.
